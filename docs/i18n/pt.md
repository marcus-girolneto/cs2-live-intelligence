# Português (Brasil) (pt) glossary

Tone: informal "você"; CS terms used by the Brazilian scene. No em dashes. Brand and persona names stay as they are (FRAGMIND, FRAGMIND Coach,
Personal Coach, Pro, Basic, Free plan names, Quick, Deep). Map callouts, weapon names and stat
abbreviations (ADR, KAST, HS, K/D, AWP) are never translated.
