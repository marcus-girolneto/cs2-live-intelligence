# 简体中文 (zh) glossary

Tone: Simplified Chinese with the terms used on 5E/Perfect World servers. No em dashes. Brand and persona names stay as they are (FRAGMIND, FRAGMIND Coach,
Personal Coach, Pro, Basic, Free plan names, Quick, Deep). Map callouts, weapon names and stat
abbreviations (ADR, KAST, HS, K/D, AWP) are never translated.
