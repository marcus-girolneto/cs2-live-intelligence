# Français (fr) glossary

Tone: informal "tu"; English CS terms common in the French scene; non-breaking space before : ; ? !. No em dashes. Brand and persona names stay as they are (FRAGMIND, FRAGMIND Coach,
Personal Coach, Pro, Basic, Free plan names, Quick, Deep). Map callouts, weapon names and stat
abbreviations (ADR, KAST, HS, K/D, AWP) are never translated.
