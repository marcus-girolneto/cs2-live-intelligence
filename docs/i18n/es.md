# Español (es) glossary

Tone: neutral Spanish with "tú", understood in Spain and Latin America. No em dashes. Brand and persona names stay as they are (FRAGMIND, FRAGMIND Coach,
Personal Coach, Pro, Basic, Free plan names, Quick, Deep). Map callouts, weapon names and stat
abbreviations (ADR, KAST, HS, K/D, AWP) are never translated.
