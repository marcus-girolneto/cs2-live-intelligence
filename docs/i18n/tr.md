# Türkçe (tr) glossary

Tone: informal "sen"; English CS terms common in the Turkish scene with Turkish suffixes after an apostrophe. No em dashes. Brand and persona names stay as they are (FRAGMIND, FRAGMIND Coach,
Personal Coach, Pro, Basic, Free plan names, Quick, Deep). Map callouts, weapon names and stat
abbreviations (ADR, KAST, HS, K/D, AWP) are never translated.
